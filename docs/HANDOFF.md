## Product

AniBoop is an interactive collection of chibi anime mascots. The intended experience is to explore a pannable gallery, boop a character to see it react, and download that character’s direction and reaction sheets. The slogan is **Little characters. Big reactions.** The [brand guide](BRAND.md) and [shared copy](../src/lib/brand.ts) hold the approved wording. The [feature blueprint](FEATURE_BLUEPRINT.md) holds the planned interactions and acceptance criteria.

## What runs today

- The [homepage](../src/app/page.tsx) is a full-screen, pannable gallery populated by the [catalog](../src/data/characters.ts) (interleaved round-robin from per-series JSON files in [`src/data/characters/`](../src/data/characters)). The first catalog entry occupies the center cell, and the catalog repeats in spiral order. A floating bottom [GalleryDock](../src/components/gallery/gallery-dock.tsx) lets users filter the live grid to a single series (synced with `/?series=<slug>` via `nuqs`) while keeping the full catalog searchable. All mascot WebPs (`previews/`, `gallery-sheets/`, and 1080 × 1080 `sheets/`) are delivered from Cloudflare R2 (`NEXT_PUBLIC_R2_ASSETS_URL`) with one-year immutable caching and `?v=<hash>` cache busting (falling back to local `/mascots/` files when unset). Character details ("View & download") and asset downloads are available through the catalog, on each tile, or directly via `/?character=<id>` deep links (synced with `nuqs`) via lazily loaded responsive dialogs on desktop and drawers on mobile, with a pinned utility footer offering **Try in Playground** and **Copy link**. Modal overlays use a crisp `bg-black/70` scrim without backdrop blur.
- The [app header](../src/components/header/app-header.tsx) is preceded by a focus-revealed **Skip to gallery** link, keeps Catalog in reach on narrow screens, and moves secondary controls (including the Playground link) into More options. Ctrl+K or Cmd+K opens the catalog, mobile drawers include an explicit close button alongside swipe-to-dismiss, and appearance, reaction sound, and boop order settings persist across visits. Gallery instructions are available to screen readers.
- [`/playground`](../src/app/playground/page.tsx) provides a 100% client-side workbench where users can drag and drop their own 3 × 3 Directions and Reactions sprite sheets (via `URL.createObjectURL`) or pick any character from the shared `CharacterCatalog` (or open `/playground?character=<id>`, loading `1080 × 1080` sheets from R2) to preview live cursor gaze tracking, boop reactions, and reaction sounds.
- The header and social preview use the full-color logo. On the first visit in a browser tab (when motion is enabled), [`SplashScreen`](../src/components/splash/splash-screen.tsx) plays a squishy double-hop intro and docks the sticker into the interactive [brand logo](../src/components/header/brand-logo.tsx) in the header, which can also be clicked anytime for an in-place boop. The gallery background repeats the flat wordmark in the active theme, and the purple tap mark supplies browser and launcher icons. See the [brand guide](BRAND.md#logo-and-marks) and [metadata routes](ARCHITECTURE.md#fonts-and-metadata).
- `aniboop.nmrisrl.dev` is the official production origin. `aniboop.vercel.app` is the direct backup for a parent-domain outage. The [SEO guide](SEO.md#production-origin) documents the Vercel build variable and the backup procedure.
- [CharacterGrid](../src/components/gallery/character-grid.tsx) uses the local [Thiings Grid adaptation](../src/components/gallery/thiings-grid.tsx). It seeds a centered 7 × 5 window on SSR to avoid a blank first paint, derives stable spiral indices, and syncs to the visible window plus overscan on mount. Pointer input moves one translated layer via animation frames, and React updates the cell window when needed. Mouse, touch, wheel, trackpad, and arrow keys can pan. Preserve the touch pointer-capture handling and reduced-motion behavior. [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) explains the grid and links to the upstream implementation.
- A shared intersection observer decodes 248 × 248 WebP previews through [`src/lib/image-decode.ts`](../src/lib/image-decode.ts) as tiles enter view, mounting each mascot after decode completes and crossfading character and caption over its centered [bloom loader](../src/components/ui/image-loader.tsx) before unmounting the loader. An unpressed fine pointer wakes gallery-wide gaze and loads compact direction sheets for visible mascots. A boop, hold, or pinch loads the reaction sheet. Panning pauses gaze tracking, and Tab can reach a mascot when at least half its button is visible. [Gallery loading](GALLERY_ASSETS.md#gallery-loading) documents the delivery behavior.
- Reaction sounds persist across visits via local storage (defaulting to off). Enable or mute them with the speaker button or under More options. The gallery plays a synthesized cue for each displayed reaction, including hold and pinch expressions. [Reaction audio](ARCHITECTURE.md#reaction-audio) documents the playback hook and presets.
- The [Mascot component](../src/components/mascot/mascot.tsx) adapts the implementation in `../nmrisrl-v2/components/ui/mascot.tsx` and upstream `page-mascot`. It supports all nine reaction slots, configurable reaction pools, and hold and pinch gestures. The touch handler recognizes taps on release because Chromium can omit the synthesized click after a fast pan.
- The stack is Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind v4, and shadcn’s `radix-nova` style with the unified `radix-ui` dependency. [Global CSS](../src/app/globals.css) defines the light and dark palettes through semantic tokens and the `squircle:` feature-query variant (`@supports (corner-shape: squircle)`). Use classes such as `bg-card` and `text-foreground` across themes. Typography is local Allo Bold for headings, Inter Variable for UI, and system monospace. Pair a fallback `rounded-*` radius with `corner-squircle` and `squircle:rounded-*` (supplied by default on shared primitives in `src/components/ui`).

## Mascot assets and Git snapshot

Each catalog entry has five Git-ignored local WebPs (`public/mascots/sheets/`, `public/mascots/previews/`, and `public/mascots/gallery-sheets/`) mirrored to Cloudflare R2 (`aniboop-assets.nmrisrl.dev`), with an 8-character SHA-256 content hash (`v`) recorded in its series file under `src/data/characters/`. The still and compact sheets share the same framing. Run `git status --short --branch` to check the current working tree and branch.

- To add a mascot, install Pillow, NumPy, and boto3 once with `python -m pip install Pillow numpy boto3`. Put the matching 1080 × 1080 `<id>-directions` and `<id>-reactions` PNG or WebP files in `public/mascots/raw/`, then run `npm run mascot:add -- --id <id> --name "<name>" --series "<series>"`. A successful run uploads all five WebPs directly to Cloudflare R2 first, appends `{ id, name, series, v }` to the catalog, and moves originals to `raw/processed/`. Use `npm run mascot:update -- --id <id>` to replace an existing character's artwork (also uploading to R2 before refreshing `v`). [Adding a mascot](GALLERY_ASSETS.md#adding-a-mascot) and [R2 storage](R2_STORAGE.md) cover the full workflow.
- The sibling `../mascot-maker` project supplied the character metadata. Its catalog includes a fox that is excluded from AniBoop. The sibling `../threadfx` project is the reference for Oxc, editor settings, Tailwind lint, and reusable agent workflows.

## Historical verification

The 24 additions that brought the gallery to 163 characters were checked against the public CDN. All 120 versioned WebP URLs matched their local files. Earlier Chromium checks confirmed preview, gaze, and boop loading for the batch that brought the gallery to 139 characters. Chromium DevTools checks covered catalog trigger prop forwarding, form control identification, and lazy-loaded image aspect ratios. Checks after the component reorganization covered the compact header down to 320 px, landscape layout, touch and keyboard activation, and focus restoration after closing dialogs.

Earlier framing checks covered the original 16 center previews and 288 playback poses. A batch preview rebuild verified that the generator skips uncataloged source sheets when run against the project's asset directory. Archive checks covered existing destination files and a simulated catalog write failure.

A local production test in a 1440 × 900 Chromium viewport fetched 33 WebPs for opening the gallery, moving the mouse, and booping once. Repeating the visit in the same browser reused all 33 from cache with zero image bytes transferred. Browser checks also covered gaze across cards, drag followed by a touch tap, and keyboard boops with reduced motion. Physical-phone profiling and deployed cache verification remain open.

Repeated mouse boops with slight pointer movement kept neighboring mascots in their last gaze pose during each press. Chromium also verified that a drag still pans and unpressed movement resumes gaze tracking.
